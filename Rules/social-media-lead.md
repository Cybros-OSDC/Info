# Social Media & Communications Lead — Rules

- Verify information before publishing.
- Do not post confidential or private information.
- Get appropriate permission before posting someone's photo or personal information.
- Give credit to contributors, designers, speakers, and collaborators.
- Do not post offensive, misleading, discriminatory, or controversial content on behalf of OSDC.
- Do not change account credentials without informing the appropriate coordinator.
- Keep account credentials secure.
- Follow the branding guidelines approved by OSDC.
- Major announcements should be reviewed before publication.
