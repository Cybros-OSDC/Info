# OSDC Student / Member — Rules

- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone.
- No plagiarism or false claims about contributions.
- Give proper credit to others' work.
- Do not spam group discussions.
- Do not intentionally disrupt projects or events.
- Communicate early if you cannot complete an assigned task.
- Follow the OSDC Code of Conduct.
- Beginners are welcome — asking questions is encouraged.
