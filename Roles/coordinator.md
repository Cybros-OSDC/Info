# OSDC Coordinator

## Role

The OSDC Coordinator helps the team stay organized, connected, and focused on the goals of the Open Source Development Club.

## Responsibilities

- Coordinate OSDC activities, projects, and events.
- Help different teams communicate with each other.
- Assign responsibilities when required.
- Keep track of ongoing tasks and activities.
- Organize team meetings and discussions.
- Make sure important decisions are documented.
- Support team members when they face coordination issues.
- Encourage students to participate and contribute.
- Work with the Operations & Management Team on planning and execution.
- Represent OSDC when coordination with external teams is required.

## Goal

The Coordinator's goal is to make OSDC organized, welcoming, and easy for everyone to contribute to.
