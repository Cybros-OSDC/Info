# Operations & Management Team

## Role

The Operations & Management Team handles the planning and practical organization required to keep OSDC activities running smoothly.

## Responsibilities

- Help plan OSDC events and activities.
- Coordinate venues, schedules, and logistics when required.
- Maintain event-related information.
- Coordinate with different OSDC teams.
- Help manage registrations and attendance.
- Support workshops, hackathons, meetups, and other activities.
- Maintain basic records of OSDC activities.
- Coordinate requirements with the Coordinator.
- Identify operational problems and help resolve them.

## Goal

Make OSDC events and activities organized, smooth, and enjoyable for everyone involved.
