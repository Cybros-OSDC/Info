# Social Media & Communications Lead

## Role

The Social Media & Communications Lead manages OSDC's public communication across X, Instagram, and LinkedIn.

## Responsibilities

- Manage OSDC's official social-media presence.
- Create and schedule posts for:
  - X
  - Instagram
  - LinkedIn
- Promote OSDC events, workshops, projects, and achievements.
- Maintain a simple content calendar.
- Coordinate with the Design & Branding Lead for graphics.
- Coordinate with the Operations & Management Team for event announcements.
- Highlight student and contributor achievements.
- Share important OSDC updates with the community.
- Maintain a consistent and professional OSDC identity.
- Monitor engagement and improve content when needed.

## Goal

Make OSDC's work visible, professional, and approachable across social media.
