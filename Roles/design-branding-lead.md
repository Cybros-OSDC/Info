# Design & Branding Lead

## Role

The Design & Branding Lead maintains the visual quality and consistency of OSDC's public materials.

## Responsibilities

- Review posters, banners, social-media graphics, and other visual materials.
- Maintain consistent OSDC branding.
- Work with the Social Media & Communications Lead on graphics.
- Help designers understand OSDC's visual guidelines.
- Approve designs before major public announcements.
- Maintain reusable templates where useful.
- Encourage creativity while maintaining consistency.
- Coordinate with the Operations & Management Team for event-specific designs.

## Goal

Keep OSDC's public presence consistent, recognizable, and visually appealing.
